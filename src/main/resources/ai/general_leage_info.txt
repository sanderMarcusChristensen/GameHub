League of Legends is a team-based competitive game. Two teams play against each other, and each player controls a character called a champion. Teams work together to destroy the opposing team's Nexus.

A kill is recorded when a player defeats an opponent's champion. A death is recorded when that player's champion is defeated. An assist is recorded when the player contributes to defeating an opponent without getting the kill.

Kills, deaths and assists describe only part of a game. A player's role, champion and other in-game actions can affect these numbers. These general facts do not establish a specific player's role, actions or reasons for their results.
